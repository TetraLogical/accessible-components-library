What is an accessibility audit?

An audit is a detailed review of a website or application against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). It identifies the barriers people encounter, and explains how to remove them.

How long does an audit take?

It depends on the size of the product and the number of unique samples. Most audits take between two and four weeks from the start of testing to the delivery of the report.

What do we get at the end?

You get a report which lists each issue, the people it affects, the guidelines it relates to, and a recommendation for how to fix it.

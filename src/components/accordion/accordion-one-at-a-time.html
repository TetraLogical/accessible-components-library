Auditing

We review a website or application against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, and report the barriers people encounter along with a recommendation for each one. Read more on the TetraLogical website.

Training

We run workshops for designers, developers, content authors and testers, so that teams can find and fix accessibility problems themselves rather than waiting for the next audit.

Research

We carry out usability research with disabled people, so that teams understand how their product performs in practice and not only whether it meets the guidelines.
